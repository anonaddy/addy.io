## New

- Lite users can now reset bandwidth from the [subscription page](https://app.addy.io/settings/subscription). The first reset each calendar year is applied automatically at no extra cost. Further resets in the same year are a one-off payment.

## Improvements

- Made alias label modal wording clearer, so it is obvious when you are updating labels for one alias versus managing all labels on the account
- Renamed bulk "Edit Recipients" and "Edit Labels" to "Update Recipients" and "Update Labels"

## Fixes

- Fixed Outlook emails failing to forward due to a double @ in the Message-ID
- Fixed forwarding errors caused by malformed From, To or Sender headers
- Fixed uncaught 429 rate limit errors in the web app
