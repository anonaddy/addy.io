## addy.io has partnered with Leave Me Alone

We're excited to share that addy.io has partnered with [Leave Me Alone](https://leavemealone.com/), a privacy-first tool to unsubscribe from newsletters and manage your inbox.

addy.io paid subscribers can now get 10% off a 7 day pass, 45% off the Yearly Casual Plan, or 65% off the Yearly InboxZero Hero plan.

How to claim your discount

<ol>
    <li>1. Log in to your addy.io dashboard</li>
    <li>2. Click your username (top-right) > “addy.io Perks”</li>
    <li>3. Find the Leave Me Alone offer and click “Redeem code”</li>
    <li>4. Click on the link "Click to visit Leave Me Alone and apply code"</li>
</ol>
