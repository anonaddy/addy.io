## addy.io has partnered with Obscura

We're excited to share that addy.io has partnered with [Obscura](https://obscura.com/), the first VPN that can't log your activity and outsmarts internet censorship.

addy.io paid subscribers can now get 25% off Obscura for up to a year. The code works on all platforms for top-ups and subscriptions.

How to claim your discount

<ol>
    <li>1. Log in to your addy.io dashboard</li>
    <li>2. Click your username (top-right) > “addy.io Perks”</li>
    <li>3. Find the Obscura offer and click “Redeem code”</li>
    <li>4. Use the code during checkout on any platform for top-ups and subscriptions</li>
</ol>
