## addy.io has partnered with Ente

We're excited to share that addy.io has partnered with [Ente](https://ente.com/), a privacy-first, end-to-end encrypted photo and video storage service that ensures only you can access your memories.

addy.io paid subscribers can now get 25% off for the first 12 months on any Ente plan. Apply the code on photos.ente.com before you purchase. App Store and Play Store purchases cannot use this code.

How to claim your discount

<ol>
    <li>1. Log in to your addy.io dashboard</li>
    <li>2. Click your username (top-right) > “addy.io Perks”</li>
    <li>3. Find the Ente offer and click “Redeem code”</li>
    <li>4. Click the link "Click to visit Ente and apply code". Apply the code on photos.ente.com before you purchase. App Store and Play Store purchases cannot use this code.</li>
</ol>
