Alias labels let you organise your [aliases](/help/what-are-the-different-kinds-of-aliases/) with custom tags such as **Shopping**, **Banking**, **Newsletters**, or **Work**. You can assign labels when creating an alias, update labels later, filter your alias list by label, and apply labels to multiple aliases at once.

<h2 id="create-labels">Create labels</h2>

1. [Log in](https://app.addy.io) and go to **Aliases**.
2. Open either:
   - **Update Alias Labels** for a single alias, or
   - **Update Labels for Aliases** after selecting multiple aliases.
3. Click **Click here to manage labels**.
4. In **Manage labels**, enter a **Name**, choose a **Colour**, then click **Create**.

You can create labels once and reuse them across many aliases.

<h2 id="assign-labels-to-a-new-alias">Assign labels to a new alias</h2>

1. In **Aliases**, click **Create alias**.
2. In the **Labels** field, select one or more labels (optional).
3. Click **Create Alias**.

The new alias is created with those labels already attached.

<h2 id="assign-or-change-labels-for-an-existing-alias">Assign or change labels for an existing alias</h2>

1. In **Aliases**, find the alias you want to update.
2. In the **Labels** column, click the edit icon.
3. Select or remove labels.
4. Click **Update Labels**.

You can assign up to **10 labels per alias**.

<h2 id="update-labels-for-multiple-aliases">Update labels for multiple aliases</h2>

1. In **Aliases**, tick the checkboxes for the aliases you want to update.
2. Choose **Update Labels for Aliases**.
3. Select the labels you want to apply.
4. Click **Update Labels**.

This is useful when tagging many aliases at once (for example, marking older aliases as **Archive**).

<h2 id="filter-aliases-by-label">Filter aliases by label</h2>

Use the **Label filter** dropdown at the top of the aliases list to show:

- **All labels**, or
- Only aliases with a specific label.

This makes it easier to review groups of aliases without changing their domains, recipients, or status.

<h2 id="edit-or-delete-labels">Edit or delete labels</h2>

1. Open **Manage labels** from an alias labels modal.
2. To rename or recolour a label, click **Edit**, update the values, then click **Save**.
3. To remove a label, click **Delete** and confirm.

Deleting a label removes it from all aliases that currently use it.
