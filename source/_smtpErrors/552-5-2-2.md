## What it means

The recipient's mailbox has run out of space, or the account has gone over a limit. The standard name for this status is "mailbox full".

The `552` reply code makes it a permanent rejection for this message, so the sender won't retry it. Many sending services treat a full mailbox more gently than a missing one, but several of these bounces in a row can still get the address suppressed. The temporary version is [452 4.2.2](/smtp-errors/452-4-2-2/).

## Common causes

- The inbox has hit its storage limit. Large attachments in Sent or Archive folders often count too.
- The account has gone over a sending, receiving or bandwidth limit for the month.
- A shared or team mailbox that nobody has cleared out.
- The account has been abandoned. Gmail adds "and inactive" when a full inbox also hasn't been used for a long time, which usually means nobody is reading it.

## If you're the recipient

Free up space by deleting large emails and emptying Trash and Spam, or upgrade your storage with your email provider. Then ask anyone whose email bounced to send it again. The bounced emails won't arrive by themselves.

## If you're the sender

Wait a day or two and send again, or contact the person another way to let them know their mailbox is full. If you send bulk email, check how your sending service handles full-mailbox bounces so the address isn't removed permanently after one bounce.

## If you use addy.io

addy.io sends `552 5.2.2 Recipient address rejected: User over quota` when an account on the Free plan has gone over its monthly bandwidth limit. Forwarding starts again when your bandwidth resets at the start of the next calendar month<span data-pricing>, or straight away if you upgrade</span>. See [What happens if I go over my bandwidth limit](/faq/#what-happens-if-i-go-over-my-bandwidth-limit-in-a-given-month).
