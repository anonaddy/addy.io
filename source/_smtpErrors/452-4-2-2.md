## What it means

The recipient's mailbox is full or over a limit, but the receiving server is asking the sender to try again later instead of bouncing the email. The standard name for this status is "mailbox full".

Because it's temporary, the sending server keeps the email in its queue and retries. Most servers keep trying for somewhere between one and five days before giving up and sending a bounce.

## If you're the recipient

Free up space, or deal with whatever limit you've hit, as soon as you can. Any email still being retried will then be delivered on the next attempt. Once the sender's server gives up, that email won't arrive, and the sender has to send it again.

## If you're the sender

You don't need to do anything yet, as your server is retrying automatically. If it eventually bounces, contact the person another way to let them know their mailbox is full.

## If you use addy.io

addy.io sends `452 4.2.2 Recipient address rejected: User over quota` when an account on the Lite plan has gone over its monthly bandwidth limit after using its free reset for the year. Senders keep retrying while addy.io defers the email.

Wait for the next calendar month<span data-pricing>, reset your bandwidth from your subscription page or upgrade to Pro</span>, and any email still being retried can then be delivered.
