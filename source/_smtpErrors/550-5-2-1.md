## What it means

The mailbox exists, but it isn't accepting mail right now. The standard name for this status is "mailbox disabled, not accepting messages".

Some servers send it with a `550` reply code and others with `552`, but the meaning is the same.

## Common causes

- The account has been suspended or disabled by the email provider, often after a long period of not being used.
- The account was closed and the address is being held before it's deleted.
- The account owner or an administrator has blocked incoming mail.

## If you're the recipient

Log in to the account and check for messages from your email provider about a suspension. Free providers often disable accounts that haven't been signed in to for a long time. Once the account is active again, ask senders to resend, and check they haven't suppressed your address after the bounce.

## If you're the sender

Treat it like a hard bounce. Stop sending to the address and contact the person another way if you need to reach them.

## If you use addy.io

addy.io sends `552 5.2.1 Recipient address rejected: User disabled` when the whole addy.io account has been disabled. If you see this for an alias that you own, please [get in touch](/contact/).

If the error comes from your own inbox provider when addy.io forwards an email to you, it appears on your Failed Deliveries page. Check your inbox account is still active, or [add a different recipient](/help/adding-a-recipient/).
