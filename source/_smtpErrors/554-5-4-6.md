## What it means

The email has passed between mail servers so many times that a server decided it must be going round in circles and stopped it. Each server adds a `Received:` header as it handles an email, and once there are too many, usually 50 or more, the email is rejected. The standard name for this status is "routing loop detected".

## Common causes

- Two addresses forward to each other, such as an inbox that forwards to an alias, which forwards back to the same inbox.
- An auto-forward rule sends mail to an address that has its own forward back again.
- A domain's MX records point to a server that sends the mail back to where it came from.
- An auto-reply or vacation message replying to another auto-reply.

## If you're the recipient

Check your forwarding rules, and those on any address you forward to. Look for a chain that leads back to the start, and remove one link from it.

## If you're the sender

There's usually nothing wrong on your side. Let the recipient know their forwarding is set up in a loop.

## If you use addy.io

addy.io rejects looping emails with `554 5.4.0 Error: too many hops`. This usually means your recipient inbox, or a rule in it, forwards mail back to one of your aliases. Remove that forward, or [change the alias's recipient](/help/changing-the-recipients-for-an-alias/) so the chain ends at an inbox that doesn't forward.
