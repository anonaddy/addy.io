## What it means

Microsoft's servers, used by Outlook.com, Hotmail and Microsoft 365, refused the email because they've blocked the sending server or the sender. The block isn't about the recipient, so every Microsoft address rejects it in the same way.

Microsoft uses a range of related codes:

- **`5.7.606` to `5.7.649`** - the sending IP address is on Microsoft's own blocklist.
- **`5.7.708`** - Microsoft doesn't accept mail from that IP at all, usually because it has a poor reputation or belongs to a new or trial Microsoft 365 tenant.
- **`5.7.501`, `5.7.511` and other "banned sender" codes** - the sender's address or domain has been blocked for spam.

## If you're the recipient

There's nothing to change on your side, as you can't allowlist your way past these blocks. Let the sender know, and pass on the full error message if you can.

## If you're the sender

For `5.7.606` to `5.7.649`, you can ask Microsoft to remove your IP address with its delist portal at `sender.office.com`, once you've fixed whatever caused the spam. `5.7.708` usually can't be delisted that way, so send through an established email provider, or ask your current provider for a different IP. For banned sender codes, contact Microsoft support.

## If you use addy.io

If your Outlook or Microsoft 365 inbox rejects an email that addy.io forwards with one of these codes, it appears on your Failed Deliveries page, and the block is on addy.io's sending IP. Please [get in touch](/contact/) with the full error, and consider [adding a different recipient](/help/adding-a-recipient/) in the meantime.
